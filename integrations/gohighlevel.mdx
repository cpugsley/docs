CloudConcierge integrates with **GoHighLevel** in two modes. Pick the one that matches your situation.

## Two modes

### Mode 1: BYOC (Bring Your Own GHL)

You have an existing GHL account/sub-account. CloudConcierge connects via OAuth and pushes call data into your CRM.

**Available on:** all plans

### Mode 2: Managed GHL

You don't have GHL. CloudConcierge **provisions a sub-account for you** in our Agency account and pre-configures pipelines, calendar, custom fields. White-labeled — appears as "CloudConcierge CRM" to you, no GHL branding visible.

**Available on:** Professional and Business plans only (included)

## GHL is unique: CRM + Calendar in one

Unlike HubSpot, GHL implements both CRM and Calendar interfaces in CloudConcierge. If you use Managed GHL:

- No need to connect Google or Microsoft Calendar separately
- The GHL calendar serves as your scheduling system AND your CRM
- One integration, one place to look

If you prefer keeping your existing Google/Outlook calendar and just using GHL for CRM, you can — see "Calendar choice" below.

## BYOC setup

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings → Integrations">
    Find **GoHighLevel** in the CRM section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose 'Connect my existing GHL account'">
    From the GHL onboarding dialog.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in to GHL via OAuth">
    Pick the GHL location (sub-account) to connect.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and accept permissions">
    See "Required scopes" below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure mapping">
    CRM Config panel appears for tuning.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Required scopes (BYOC)
- `contacts.readonly` `contacts.write` — read and create contacts
- `opportunities.readonly` `opportunities.write` — create deals when appointments book
- `calendars.readonly` `calendars.write` — for calendar integration
- `calendars/events.readonly` `calendars/events.write` — for booking appointments
- `locations.readonly` — read your sub-account info

## Managed setup

<Steps>
  <Step title="Subscribe to Professional or Business">
    Managed GHL is included; not available on Starter.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Settings → Integrations">
    Find **GoHighLevel**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose 'Set up a new CRM for me'">
    From the GHL onboarding dialog.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Provisioning runs (takes ~30 seconds)">
    Progress shown live: "Creating your CRM... Setting up calendar... Configuring pipeline... Adding custom fields..."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Done">
    Your GHL sub-account is ready. CloudConcierge has full access via Agency credentials — no OAuth needed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### What gets pre-configured (Managed)

- **Location** (GHL term for sub-account) — your business name, address, timezone
- **Calendar** — default calendar named after your business
- **Pipeline:** "Inbound Leads" with stages New Lead → Appointment Scheduled → Completed → Won/Lost
- **Custom fields** — `cc_call_sentiment`, `cc_call_tags`, `cc_source`, `cc_last_call_summary`
- **Default automations** — none. We don't pre-build workflows; you can if you want.

## Calendar choice

If you have an existing calendar connected (Google or Microsoft):

When you set up GHL, CC will ask:
- **Use GHL calendar** — replaces your existing primary calendar
- **Use GHL for CRM only** — keep Google/Outlook as primary, GHL handles CRM

**Default:** for fresh accounts, GHL calendar. For existing accounts with another calendar, "CRM only" (don't break what works).

## What syncs to GHL

After every call:

1. **Contact** created (or matched if existing) by phone
2. **Note** added to contact with AI summary, sentiment, tags, recording URL
3. **Opportunity** created in pipeline (if "Create deal on appointment" enabled and call resulted in booking)

## CRM Config panel

Same as HubSpot — toggle which fields sync, pick the pipeline + default stage for opportunities. Pipelines and stages are fetched live from your GHL account.

## Managed GHL cost

Built into your Professional (\$199/mo) or Business (\$349/mo) plan. No extra fee. You get an unrestricted GHL sub-account with no platform-fee passthrough.

If you cancel CloudConcierge:
- Your sub-account is **suspended** immediately (data preserved)
- After 30 days, the sub-account is deleted (carrier release)
- You can export contacts during the 30-day window — contact support

## Disconnecting

**BYOC mode:** Settings → Integrations → GoHighLevel → Disconnect. Standard OAuth revoke.

**Managed mode:** can't disconnect without canceling the CC subscription (it's tied to your plan). To "disconnect" Managed GHL, downgrade to Starter — sub-account is auto-suspended.

## Troubleshooting

**Provisioning fails with "Failed to create location"**
- Our GHL Agency API has rare hiccups. Click **Retry Provisioning**; usually works the second time.
- If retry fails too, contact support — we can provision manually.

**Pipelines empty in CRM Config dropdown**
- Verify your GHL location actually has pipelines (Managed accounts get the "Inbound Leads" pipeline by default; BYOC accounts use whatever you have)
- Refresh the dashboard; pipeline list is cached for 5 minutes

**Contacts not landing in GHL**
- Wait 2-3 min (CRM push is async)
- Check **Settings → Integrations → CRM Health** widget for sync errors
- If errors persist, contact support
