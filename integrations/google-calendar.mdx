CloudConcierge can read your Google Calendar to know when you're free, and write to it when callers book appointments. The integration uses OAuth — your data stays in your Google account, we just hold a refresh token.

## Connect Google Calendar

1. Open **Settings → Integrations**
2. Find **Google Calendar** in the Calendar Integrations section
3. Click **Connect**
4. A Google sign-in popup appears
5. Sign in with the Google account that owns the calendar you want CloudConcierge to use
6. Review the requested permissions:
   - **See and edit events on all your calendars** — required for booking
   - **See your email address** — used to label which Google account is connected
   - **See your basic profile info** — used to display your name in the dashboard
7. Click **Allow**
8. Popup closes; the integration card shows **Connected ✓**

## Pick a primary calendar

After connecting, choose which calendar to use:

- **Personal calendar** (recommended for solo operators)
- **Work calendar** (recommended if it's a separate calendar)
- **Dedicated CloudConcierge calendar** (recommended if you want a clean separation)

In **Settings → Integrations → Google Calendar**, click **Set as primary calendar**. Bookings now land on that calendar.

<Tip>
If you're a solo operator and your personal Google Calendar already shows your true availability, use it directly. CloudConcierge respects existing events when offering availability to callers.
</Tip>

## What the AI does

**During a call:**
- Caller asks "can I book an appointment Tuesday?" → AI checks your calendar live
- AI offers available slots based on your business hours + existing events
- Caller picks a time → AI confirms details and writes the event to your calendar

**Event details:**
- Title: configurable per appointment type (e.g., "Drain cleaning — John Doe")
- Description: includes caller name, phone, notes, and a link back to the call recording
- Duration: per appointment type (default: 30 minutes)

## Disconnecting

To revoke access:

1. **Settings → Integrations → Google Calendar**
2. Click **Disconnect**
3. Confirm

You should also revoke the OAuth grant in [Google account settings](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions) for completeness.

## Troubleshooting

**"Access Blocked" error during connect**
- This was an issue in early dev — fixed in production. If you see this now, contact support@cloudconcierge.ai.

**Bookings not appearing on calendar**
- Check **Settings → Integrations** → is Google Calendar set as primary?
- Check that the OAuth grant is still valid (Google revokes after long inactivity)
- Disconnect + reconnect to refresh the token

**Tokens expiring frequently**
- Our Google OAuth app is in "Testing" mode while we await production approval. In Testing mode, refresh tokens expire every ~6 days. This is expected during early access. Production approval will resolve.

</Note>
