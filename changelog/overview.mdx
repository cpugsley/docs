<Update label="May 22, 2026">

## New features

**Scheduled report emails**
You can now subscribe to daily and weekly digest emails that summarize your call activity. Daily digests arrive between 5–7 PM in your business timezone with yesterday's totals, bookings, sentiment, and notable calls. Weekly digests land Monday morning (7–9 AM local) with week-over-week trends, booking conversion rates, peak call hours, and top failed queries. Manage subscriptions per team member under **Account → Notifications**. Learn more in [Scheduled reports](/concepts/scheduled-reports).

</Update>
