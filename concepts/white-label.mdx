If you're a marketing agency, MSP, IT consultancy, or vertical-specific platform serving SMBs, you can resell CloudConcierge under your own brand. Your clients see your name, your domain, your branding — never CloudConcierge.

## What "white-label" means at CloudConcierge

```
                   YOU (Reseller)
                     │
                     ├─── Brand: "Acme Calls" (your name)
                     ├─── Domain: calls.acmeagency.com
                     ├─── Pricing: your margin
                     │
            ┌────────┴────────┐
            │                 │
        Client 1          Client 2
        Sees "Acme        Sees "Acme
        Calls"            Calls"
```

You become a reseller. CC stays invisible to your end clients.

## What's included (Reseller tier)

- **White-labeled dashboard** at your domain (e.g., `calls.acmeagency.com`)
- **Custom branding:** logo, colors, fonts, favicons applied throughout
- **Reseller admin panel** — manage all your client tenants from one view
- **Volume pricing** — discounted CC infrastructure cost; you set your own client pricing
- **Co-branded docs** — your branding on `docs.acmeagency.com` (auto-generated from our docs)
- **Custom support email** — `support@acmeagency.com` instead of `support@cloudconcierge.ai`
- **Per-client billing** — bill your clients however you want; we bill you for the aggregate

## What's NOT white-labeled

For technical/compliance reasons, the following stay CloudConcierge:

- **The actual AI model providers** (AWS Bedrock, Claude, etc.) — you can't claim you built the AI
- **Underlying telephony** (Amazon Connect, Twilio fallback) — appears in carrier logs as AWS-routed
- **Email sender (some scenarios)** — automated emails may still show "via cloudconcierge.ai" in headers
- **Voice options** — the same voice library is shared across all CC instances

What clients see in normal use: your brand. What they could find with deep technical inspection: AWS/CC infrastructure. This is fine for 99.9% of use cases.

## Pricing for resellers

Two pricing approaches:

### A) Bring Your Own Contracts (BYOC pricing)
- You pay CC at agency tier (~\$2,500/mo base + per-tenant fees)
- You charge clients whatever you want
- You manage all client billing yourself
- Highest margin, most operational work

### B) Wholesale + per-tenant
- You pay CC base reseller fee (~\$500/mo)
- We charge \$X per active client tenant (lower than retail)
- You bill clients separately at your retail price
- Lower margin, less billing operations

Specifics shift — contact partnerships@cloudconcierge.ai for current numbers.

## Onboarding clients

You have a control panel that lets you:

1. **Provision a client tenant** in 30 seconds (name, business email, plan)
2. **Configure their default settings** (services, hours, voice) — either by importing from a template or filling per-client
3. **Send them an invite link** to access the white-labeled dashboard
4. **Manage their account from your reseller panel** — change plan, view usage, impersonate (read-only)

## Per-client white-label config

Each client tenant can have **further customization** beyond your reseller branding:

- Their own business name, logo, address shown in the dashboard
- Per-tenant brand colors (override the default reseller theme)
- Per-tenant subdomain (e.g., `acme-client.your-platform.com`)

This is rarely used at the per-client level — most resellers brand it once at the agency level and use that for all clients.

## Client-facing docs

Your `docs.acmeagency.com` is auto-generated from our docs (this site you're reading), with:

- Your branding/colors
- "CloudConcierge" replaced with your product name
- Your support email
- Your dashboard URL

You can override individual pages with your own content — useful for industry-specific guidance or terminology.

## Support model

You're the front line. We're your back stop.

- **Your clients** email `support@acmeagency.com` (or whatever you set)
- **You handle** Tier 1 (knowledge base questions, configuration help, billing questions)
- **You escalate to us** for Tier 2 (technical bugs, AI behavior issues, infrastructure)

We have a dedicated Slack channel with reseller partners for fast escalation.

## SLAs

| Tier | Response time | Resolution target |
|---|---|---|
| Critical (outage, calls not being answered) | 1 hour | 4 hours |
| High (specific tenant broken) | 4 hours | 1 business day |
| Medium (feature degraded) | 1 business day | 5 business days |
| Low (questions, enhancements) | 5 business days | Best effort |
