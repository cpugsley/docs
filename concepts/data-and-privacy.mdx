CloudConcierge handles sensitive customer data: phone calls, transcripts, recordings, customer info. We take privacy seriously and want you to know exactly what happens to that data.

## What we collect

### Call data
- **Phone number** of the caller (E.164 format)
- **Audio recording** of the call (yours and theirs)
- **Transcript** (AI-generated text of what was said)
- **AI summary** of the conversation
- **Sentiment analysis** and intent tagging (AI-derived)
- **Caller name + email** (if shared during the call)
- **Caller address** (if shared, e.g., for service appointments)

### Account data
- **Your contact info:** name, email, business address, phone
- **Your business info:** services, FAQs, hours, custom instructions
- **Payment info:** card details stored in Stripe, not in CloudConcierge
- **Integration credentials:** OAuth tokens for Google/Microsoft/HubSpot/GHL

## Where it's stored

| Data type | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Account info | Amazon DynamoDB (US East 1) | Encrypted at rest with AWS KMS |
| Call recordings | Amazon S3 | Encrypted, presigned-URL access only |
| Transcripts + summaries | DynamoDB + S3 | Encrypted |
| OAuth tokens | DynamoDB | Encrypted with KMS, restricted IAM access |
| Payment info | Stripe (not CC) | PCI Level 1 certified |
| Email content (support tickets) | Amazon Connect Cases + SES | AWS-encrypted |

All data hosted in **US-East-1 (Virginia)**. We don't replicate to other regions or other countries.

## How long we keep it

| Data | Default retention |
|---|---|
| Call recordings | 90 days, then auto-deleted |
| Call transcripts + summaries | 12 months |
| Account metadata (services, FAQs) | Indefinite, until account closure |
| Payment records | 7 years (US tax law) |
| Audit logs (admin actions) | 12 months |
| Webhook delivery logs | 30 days |
| Email support history | 24 months |

For longer recording retention (1 year, 3 years), contact support — available on Business plan.

For shorter retention (e.g., 30 days for compliance), contact support — available on all plans.

## Who has access

### Inside CloudConcierge
- **Our engineering team:** indirect access via AWS console for debugging. Audit-logged.
- **Our support team:** can impersonate your tenant (read-only) to help with issues. Audit-logged with "viewed as" attribution.
- **Our billing systems:** automated, no human eyes on payment data.

We **don't** sell your data. We **don't** share it with third parties for advertising. We use call data internally to improve the AI's accuracy, in aggregate, anonymized form.

### Outside CloudConcierge
- **Stripe:** payment data only
- **Mintlify:** the docs site you're reading — no customer data, only published docs
- **Amazon (AWS):** our infrastructure host — they don't have access to our customer data; we're their customer
- **OpenAI / Anthropic / AWS Bedrock:** AI model providers. Per-call data is sent for processing but **not retained** for model training (we use opt-out APIs).

## How AI uses call data

When the AI is on a call:
1. Audio streams to AWS for transcription
2. Transcript sent to AWS Bedrock (Claude) for response generation
3. Response synthesized back to audio and played

**Important:** AWS Bedrock does NOT train its models on customer prompts/responses (verified in their privacy policy). We explicitly use opt-out APIs.

## Customer requests

Your tenants' customers (the people calling your business) have privacy rights too.

### "Stop recording me"
If a caller asks the AI to stop recording, the AI:
- Acknowledges the request
- Transcript continues (text, not audio)
- Audio recording stops for the rest of the call
- Pre-request portion of the call is preserved

This is configurable per tenant — you can set "stop recording on request" to be the default, or require explicit confirmation.

### "Delete my data"
If a caller asks for their data deleted:
- Direct them to email support@cloudconcierge.ai with their phone number
- We delete their CallerProfile, recordings, and transcripts within 30 days
- We're required to keep call records for 90 days for our own debugging — we can shorten this on request with valid reason

### GDPR / CCPA
We're GDPR-compliant for EU callers and CCPA-compliant for California callers. Verification process for data requests:
1. Caller emails support with their phone number
2. We send a confirmation code to that number
3. They confirm via SMS
4. We process the deletion

## Closing your account

When you cancel CloudConcierge:
- 30-day grace period (covered in [Cancellation](/billing/cancellation))
- After grace period:
  - Call recordings: deleted
  - Transcripts: deleted
  - Knowledge base content: deleted
  - CallerProfiles (CC's local CRM cache): deleted
  - Account info: anonymized after legal retention period (7 years for tax records)

External integrations are **not** affected:
- Your HubSpot data stays in HubSpot
- Your Google Calendar events stay in Google Calendar
- Your GHL data stays in GHL (Managed: see below)

For Managed GHL: your sub-account is deleted on cancellation (after 30-day grace). Export your GHL data during the grace period if you want to keep it.

## Encryption details (for the technically curious)

- **At rest:** AWS KMS-encrypted S3 buckets + DynamoDB tables. Per-tenant keys for some sensitive fields (OAuth tokens).
- **In transit:** TLS 1.2+ for all customer-facing endpoints (HTTPS only)
- **Database:** DynamoDB with encryption at rest, IAM-restricted access
- **Recordings:** S3 server-side encryption (SSE-S3), presigned URLs valid 1 hour max
- **OAuth tokens:** field-level encryption with KMS before storing in DynamoDB
