CloudConcierge sends two types of recurring digest emails:

- **Daily digest** — once per day, summary of yesterday's call activity
- **Weekly digest** — once per week (Mondays), summary of the past 7 days

This page explains exactly when they arrive, how timezones work, and how to control your subscriptions.

## Delivery windows

| Digest | Delivery window | Day |
|---|---|---|
| Daily | 5:00 PM – 6:59 PM | Every day |
| Weekly | 7:00 AM – 8:59 AM | Mondays only |

Times are in **your business timezone** (configured under Settings, General). If your timezone is `America/Los_Angeles`, the daily digest arrives between 5–7 PM Pacific. If you're in `Europe/London`, it's 5–7 PM London time. Daylight Saving Time is handled automatically.

## What's in each digest

### Daily digest
Sent after the close of business so you can review the day:

- Total calls handled
- New bookings made
- Sentiment breakdown (positive / neutral / negative)
- Top intents (booking, inquiry, complaint, etc.)
- Notable calls (negative sentiment or unresolved)
- Links to dive into specific calls

### Weekly digest
Sent Monday morning so you can plan the week:

- Week-over-week trend on call volume
- Booking conversion rate
- Top failed queries (questions the AI struggled with — your FAQ backlog)
- Hours of peak call volume
- Comparison to your account average

## How the timing actually works

A bit more detail for the curious:

```
            ┌──────────────────────────────────┐
            │   Hourly cron at minute 0 UTC    │
            └──────────────┬───────────────────┘
                           │  every hour
                           ▼
            ┌──────────────────────────────────┐
            │  For every digest subscriber:    │
            │  1. Convert UTC to tenant local  │
            │     time (DST-safe)              │
            │  2. Is local time in the         │
            │     delivery window?             │
            │  3. Have we already sent today?  │
            │  4. If yes/no/no → dispatch      │
            └──────────────┬───────────────────┘
                           │  if conditions met
                           ▼
            ┌──────────────────────────────────┐
            │  Aggregate call data + send      │
            │  email via SES                   │
            │  Mark date sent (prevents dupes) │
            └──────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Why an hour-wide window?** Because the scheduler runs once per hour, your digest arrives anywhere within the 2-hour window. Most tenants get it around 5:00–5:15 PM local. The window provides slack for AWS scheduler jitter, DST edge cases, and brief outages — you'll never miss a digest because of a 15-minute hiccup.

**Why Mondays for weekly?** Most people review the prior week first thing Monday. Tuesday felt too late. Sunday felt too soon.

## Subscribing or unsubscribing

Open **Account, Notifications** (top-right user menu).

For each digest type, toggle the email checkbox:

- **Daily digest — email** (default: on for owners)
- **Weekly digest — email** (default: on for owners)

Changes take effect immediately. You can subscribe and unsubscribe per team member — each user manages their own notification preferences. Owners and team members all see the same content; we just deliver one email per subscribed recipient.

Unsubscribing from a digest does NOT affect other notifications (call summary, missed call, etc.). Those are independent toggles.

## When you might not receive a digest

A digest does NOT arrive if:

1. **No subscribers configured.** Account, Notifications shows your toggles but they have to be saved. If you're brand new and never visited the Notifications page, defaults apply but check that they're actually saved as set.
2. **No call activity for the period.** We skip empty digests — if you had zero calls yesterday, no daily digest goes out. (Some tenants prefer this; let support know if you want the empty-digest behavior changed.)
3. **Email delivery failed.** Check your spam folder. If repeatedly bouncing, your account is flagged and we email you about it.
4. **Your timezone is unset.** Defaults to `America/New_York` if blank. Set it in Settings, General to get correct local-time delivery.

## Changing delivery time

The delivery windows (5–7 PM daily, Monday 7–9 AM weekly) are fixed. We picked them based on what most SMB operators reported as the time they actually have a minute to read email.

If you'd like configurable delivery times, vote on the feature request or contact support. Once enough customers ask, we'll prioritize it.

## Retroactive digests

We don't backfill missed digests. If your account was suspended for a day or you only just turned on the subscription, you start receiving digests from today forward. Past data is always available in **Dashboard, Analytics**.
