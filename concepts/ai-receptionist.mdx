description: "What CloudConcierge's AI does on every call, and how it stays accurate."
---

## On every call, the AI:

1. **Greets the caller** with your custom greeting in your chosen voice
2. **Listens for intent** — booking, question, urgent issue, or general
3. **Answers from your knowledge base** — services, FAQs, hours
4. **Books appointments** if you've connected a calendar
5. **Transfers** to a human if the caller asks or the AI determines it should
6. **Summarizes the call** with caller info, intent, and any actions taken
7. **Sends you a notification** so you know what happened

## Accuracy

The AI uses your knowledge base as ground truth. It won't make up services, prices, or policies you haven't told it about. If a caller asks something you haven't documented, the AI offers to take a message or transfer them.

## Privacy

Calls are recorded for quality and to generate summaries. Recordings are stored in your account and accessible from the dashboard. You can delete them anytime.

## Languages

The AI matches the caller's language when possible (English, Spanish, French depending on voice).
