CloudConcierge billing runs through Stripe. All payment-related operations happen via the Stripe-hosted billing portal — secure, PCI-compliant, no card data ever touches our servers.

## Supported payment methods

- **Credit / debit card** — Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover. Most common.
- **ACH (US bank transfer)** — for annual plans only, by invoice request. Contact support.
- **Invoice billing (Net 30)** — for Business plan annual, by request. Contact support.

## Updating your card

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Billing → Update Payment Method">
    Owner role required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Redirects to Stripe portal">
    Secure form hosted by Stripe.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter new card details">
    Card number, expiration, CVC, zip.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    New card becomes default; old card removed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Changes take effect immediately. Your next subscription charge and any pending overage will use the new card.

## Multiple cards / backup card

Stripe supports multiple cards on file. We don't expose this in our dashboard yet — if you need a backup card for failover, contact support@cloudconcierge.ai and we can add it via the Stripe portal.

## Failed payments

When a charge fails (declined, insufficient funds, expired card, etc.):

```
Attempt 1 (day 0)      Failure → status: past_due
       │
       ▼ 3 days later
Attempt 2 (day 3)      Failure → reminder email sent
       │
       ▼ 5 days later
Attempt 3 (day 8)      Failure → reminder email
       │
       ▼ 7 days later
Attempt 4 (day 15)     Failure → final reminder
       │
       ▼
Day 30: status flips to suspended
         AI receptionist stops answering calls
```

You have **30 days** to fix the payment from when the first charge fails. During that window:
- Dashboard shows a red banner: "Payment failed — update your card"
- Service continues (we don't suspend until day 30)
- Multiple emails sent to your billing email + tenant owner email

Fix the card **anytime** during the 30-day window to resume normal billing. The next attempted charge usually succeeds within 1 hour of card update.

## Suspension

If 30 days pass without a successful payment:
- Account status: `restricted`
- AI receptionist stops answering calls
- Dashboard still accessible (read-only)
- Phone number still reserved (we don't release immediately)

Pay the outstanding balance + update card → service resumes within ~1 hour.

If 60 more days pass without resolution → account moves to cancellation flow → 30-day grace → number released.

So the total worst-case timeline from first failed payment to lost number: ~120 days. Plenty of time to react.

## Billing email

Where invoices and payment-failure notifications go:

**Settings → Billing → Billing Email.**

Default: your owner email. You can change to a different email (e.g., bookkeeper@yourbusiness.com) — useful if billing should go to a different person than the operator.

We send to this email:
- Receipts after successful charges
- Payment failure notifications
- Plan change confirmations
- Cancellation confirmations

We don't send marketing email to this address — only billing-related.

## Invoices

**Billing → Invoices** lists all past charges:
- Date
- Description (subscription / overage / one-time)
- Amount
- Status (paid / refunded / failed)
- Download PDF

Useful for bookkeeping. Each invoice has a downloadable PDF compatible with most accounting systems.

## Auto-pay vs manual pay

Default: **auto-pay on file**. We charge automatically on your billing cycle.

If you'd prefer manual approval (some businesses' finance policies require this) — contact support to switch to invoice billing. We'll send an invoice each cycle that you pay manually within 30 days.

## Refunds

Refunds processed back to the original card take 5-10 business days. We can't reverse this; once we initiate the refund, Stripe handles the timing.

For ACH or wire refunds, contact support — these are processed manually and take 7-14 business days.

## Trial: no payment method required

During your 14-day trial, no card is required. We ask for a card only when:
- Trial ends and you continue with a paid plan
- You upgrade from trial to a paid plan before trial expiration

If your trial expires without a payment method, the account is suspended (read-only) until you add one.

## Security

- All card data stored in Stripe (PCI Level 1 certified)
- CloudConcierge **never** stores card numbers
- All payment operations go through Stripe-hosted UI (we can't even build a form that touches card data even if we wanted to)
- Stripe customer IDs are stored in our DynamoDB; that's the link between your account and your payment data

If you have security questions about how billing data is handled, see our [data and privacy](/concepts/data-and-privacy) page or email support.
