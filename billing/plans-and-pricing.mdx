## What counts as a minute

- AI handling time on a call (the bot is listening or talking)
- Transfer time once the call is connected to a human
- Voicemail capture time

What does NOT count:
- Hold time (caller waiting before AI picks up)
- Outbound demo calls you initiate
- Internal tests from the dashboard

## Overage

If you exceed your included minutes, overage bills at the per-minute rate above. **Overage is invoiced monthly even on annual plans** — so you'll never see a big surprise bill at renewal.

Example: Professional plan annual ($159/mo), uses 650 minutes one month:
- Included: 500 min
- Overage: 150 min × $0.40 = $60
- That month's bill: $60 (overage). The next month's annual prorate has already been paid up front.

## Free trial

All new accounts get a **14-day free trial** on any plan. No credit card required during the trial. We charge on day 15 unless you cancel.

## Annual savings

Switching from monthly to annual saves ~20%. You can switch at any time. If you switch mid-month, we prorate the remaining balance.

## Plan switching

- **Upgrade** at any time. New minutes prorated; effective immediately.
- **Downgrade** at the end of your current billing period (avoids surprise minute cuts mid-cycle).

See [Switching plans](/billing/switching-plans) for the step-by-step.

## What happens if I cancel?

- During trial: no charge, account closes immediately
- After trial: account continues until end of paid period, then closes
- Your business phone number is reserved for 30 days post-cancellation in case you change your mind (we call this the "grace period")

<Note>
Phone number reservation during grace period is configurable per environment. In production, we hold numbers for 30 days; after that, they're released back to the carrier.
</Note>
