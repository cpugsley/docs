CloudConcierge plans include a minute allowance. Past that, you pay per-minute overage. We bill overage **monthly** even if you're on an annual plan — so there's never a surprise at renewal.

## How overage works

| Plan | Included minutes | Overage rate |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | 200/mo | $0.50/min |
| Professional | 500/mo | $0.40/min |
| Business | 1,000/mo | $0.30/min |

If you use more than your included minutes in a billing month, the overage is billed at the start of the next month, alongside (or in addition to) your subscription charge.

## What counts as a minute

- AI handling time (bot listening or talking)
- Transfer time after connect-to-human
- Voicemail capture time

**Does NOT count:**
- Hold time (caller waiting before AI picks up)
- Outbound test calls from the dashboard
- Internal test calls (e.g., "Call me now to test" button)
- Failed calls (didn't connect)

Minutes are rounded up per call (call ≤ 60 seconds = 1 min).

## Annual plans + monthly overage

This is the part that confuses people on annual plans.

- **Annual subscription fee:** paid up front, locks in the discounted rate for 12 months
- **Overage:** billed monthly throughout the year, never bundled into the annual fee

**Why we do it this way:** If overage accumulated for 12 months, you could be hit with a $500-1500 overage bill at year-end. Bill shock is bad for everyone. Monthly overage keeps it predictable.

**Example:** You're on Professional Annual ($159/mo = $1,908/year).
- Year 1 day 1: charged $1,908
- Months 1-12: included 500 min/month
- January: used 600 min → 100 over → charged $40 in early February
- February: used 480 min → no overage
- March: used 800 min → 300 over → charged $120 in early April
- ...and so on

You never see overage > 1 month accumulated.

## Tracking usage

**Dashboard home** shows your current month's usage:

```
Minutes Used
342 / 500
[==============___]
```

The progress bar updates in near real-time (~15 min lag). Color cues:
- Green: <80% of included
- Yellow: 80-100%
- Red: over included (overage accruing)

## Usage alerts

We send email alerts at:

- **80% of included** — heads up, you're trending toward overage
- **100% of included** — overage is now active

These alerts are still being repaired (part of the Bug #33 notifications work). Until repaired, check the dashboard manually if usage is a concern.

## How overage is invoiced

- Overage is processed at the start of each billing month
- Single line item on your invoice: "Overage — N minutes × $X.XX = $Y.YY"
- Charged to the same payment method as your subscription
- If the charge fails, your subscription enters `past_due` — fix payment method to avoid suspension

## Estimating overage

Rough math:
- 1-min calls × N calls = N minutes (most common)
- Most CC tenants average 2-4 min per call
- Heavy chat usage: 0.5-1 min equivalent per chat session

If you're consistently 50%+ over your included minutes, **upgrade your plan** — the next tier's overage rate is lower AND the included minutes are higher. Math usually works out cheaper.

## When to upgrade

Rule of thumb: if monthly overage > $30, you're better off on the next plan tier.

- **Starter at 250 min/mo:** included 200, overage $25. Borderline.
- **Starter at 400 min/mo:** overage $100 → upgrade to Professional ($199) and overage drops to $0 (under 500). Net savings: $100 - ($199 - $99) = $0. About break-even at this volume.
- **Starter at 600 min/mo:** overage $200 → Professional at this usage = $199 + $40 overage = $239. Saved $60.

We don't auto-upgrade. Decision is yours.

## Disputing overage

If your overage bill looks wrong:

1. Check Dashboard → Analytics → Calls Per Day for the billing month
2. If the math doesn't match, contact support@cloudconcierge.ai with your tenant ID + the billing month in question
3. We can pull the per-call minute log to audit

Most "wrong" overages are actually correct but driven by:
- Forgotten ongoing demos/tests
- A spike of bot/spam calls eating minutes (we can identify and exclude these)
- An integration accidentally calling the CC line in a loop
