We don't want you to cancel — but if it's not the right fit, here's how it works. No tricks, no retention call you can't escape.

## How to cancel

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Billing → Cancel Subscription">
    Owner role required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm cancellation reason">
    Optional but helpful. Tells us what to improve.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click "Yes, cancel my subscription."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirmation email">
    You get a confirmation with cancellation effective date.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## When cancellation takes effect

**Monthly plan:** at the end of your current billing period. You keep access until then.

**Annual plan:** at the end of your current annual term. No partial refunds for annual.

**During trial (no charges yet):** immediately. Account closes same day.

You can **re-subscribe** anytime before the cancellation effective date — just click "Resume subscription" in Billing.

## What happens to your business phone number

This is the part most people care about.

```
Cancellation effective date
        │
        ▼
+-------------------+
| GRACE PERIOD      |
| 30 days           |
| Your number is    |
| reserved          |
| but unused        |
+-------------------+
        │
        ▼ (after 30 days)
+-------------------+
| Number released   |
| back to carrier   |
| Cannot be         |
| reclaimed by us   |
+-------------------+
```

**During the 30-day grace period:**
- Your number is **not** answering calls
- You can resubscribe and keep the same number
- We don't release it to anyone else

**After 30 days:**
- Number goes back to the carrier pool
- Someone else may claim it eventually
- If you resubscribe later, you'll get a new number

<Tip>
If you're cancelling because of budget but want to keep the number, contact support@cloudconcierge.ai — we can sometimes work out a "pause" arrangement where you pay a small monthly fee just to hold the number.
</Tip>

## What happens to your data

| Data type | Cancellation date | After 30-day grace |
|---|---|---|
| Call recordings | Kept | **Deleted** |
| Call transcripts | Kept | Deleted |
| Bookings history | Kept | Deleted |
| Knowledge base content | Kept | Deleted |
| CRM contacts (in CC's local CallerProfiles) | Kept | Deleted |
| CRM contacts (in HubSpot/GHL) | **NOT TOUCHED** — your CRM, your data | NOT TOUCHED |
| Managed GHL sub-account (Pro+) | Suspended | Deleted |
| Dashboard access | Read-only | Closed |
| Webhooks | Disabled at cancellation | Deleted |
| API keys | Disabled at cancellation | Deleted |

## Export your data before cancellation

We strongly recommend exporting:

1. **Call log:** Dashboard → Calls → Export CSV (filtered to "all time")
2. **Bookings:** Dashboard → Bookings → Export CSV
3. **Recordings:** Click each call → Recording tab → Download. Or contact support@cloudconcierge.ai for a bulk export (~\$0 for a few hundred recordings, larger requests by case).

You have 30 days from cancellation to do this. After that, recordings are gone.

## Reactivating after cancellation

**Within 30 days:** resubscribe in the dashboard. Same number, same data, no setup.

**After 30 days:** sign up fresh. New number, no historical data. We can transfer your knowledge base content if you contact support before cancellation — saves rebuilding.

## What about the trial?

If you cancel during your 14-day free trial:
- Account closes immediately (no grace period — there was no number activity to grace)
- No charges (you weren't billed yet)
- Knowledge base content + settings deleted within 7 days
- No data export complexity (you have nothing meaningful yet)

## Asking for a refund

We don't routinely refund — once a billing period starts, we've committed compute/storage/carrier costs for the period.

Exceptions we'll consider:
- **Service outage** that prevented you from using CC for a meaningful portion of a billing period
- **Major bug** that broke your ability to operate (e.g., calls weren't being answered, no notifications were sent, etc.)
- **Annual subscription mistakes** within 30 days of switching to annual (we can prorate back to monthly)

Email support@cloudconcierge.ai with the situation and we'll talk.

## Won't take the cancellation personally

We'd love to know why you're leaving — the cancellation reason field helps us improve. But there's no retention pressure. We respect your decision. Reach back out if your needs change.
