title: "Switching plans"
description: "Upgrade, downgrade, switch monthly to annual, and what happens to your minutes mid-cycle."
---

Open **Billing → Switch Plan** to change subscriptions. CloudConcierge handles all the math (proration, minute carry-over) automatically.

## Upgrading

**Effect:** immediate.

- New plan's minutes become available right away
- You're charged a **prorated** amount for the rest of the current billing period
- Next billing cycle is at the new plan rate

**Example:** You're on Starter ($99/mo), 15 days into a 30-day cycle. You upgrade to Professional ($199/mo).
- Half of Professional remaining = $99.50
- Half of Starter already paid = $49.50 credit
- You're charged ~$50 today
- Next month's bill: full $199 Professional

Used minutes carry over: if you used 150 minutes on Starter (out of 200), the rest of this period you have access to Professional's 500 — minus the 150 already used = 350 remaining.

## Downgrading

**Effect:** at end of current billing period.

We don't downgrade mid-cycle because it would mean cutting your included minutes below what you've already paid for. Cleaner to wait until cycle reset.

You'll see a banner: "Downgrade scheduled. Takes effect on [date]." Cancel the scheduled downgrade anytime before the date.

## Switching monthly to annual

**Effect:** immediate.

- Annual costs ~20% less per month than monthly
- We refund the unused portion of your current monthly subscription as account credit
- Annual charge applied immediately (so you'll see a bigger transaction)
- Next charge is 1 year from today

**Example:** You're 10 days into a $199/mo Professional, then switch to annual ($159/mo = $1,908/year):
- Refund: $199 × 20/30 = $133 credit
- Charge: $1,908 - $133 credit = $1,775 today
- Next charge: 1 year from today, $1,908

Annual subs still get overage billed monthly — see [overage billing](/billing/overage).

## Switching annual to monthly

**Effect:** at end of current annual term.

We don't refund partial annual periods. Your annual continues until renewal, at which point we switch you to the monthly rate.

If you want to switch immediately, contact support@cloudconcierge.ai. We can pro-rate manually but generally don't.

## During trial

You're in your 14-day free trial. You can switch plans freely:
- No charges during trial
- Trial day count stays the same regardless of plan
- The plan you're on when day 15 arrives is the one we charge for

If you started on Professional but realize Starter is enough, switch to Starter on day 13 and the first charge will be $99 (not $199).

## What happens to your CC infrastructure on plan changes

| Resource | Upgrade | Downgrade |
|---|---|---|
| Business phone number | Kept | Kept |
| AI receptionist | Kept, no interruption | Kept, no interruption |
| Knowledge base content | Kept | Kept |
| Custom instructions | Kept | Kept |
| Managed GHL sub-account | Kept (Pro+ only — created if upgrading from Starter) | Suspended if downgrading to Starter (30-day grace before deletion) |
| Team members | Kept | If over new plan's limit, members keep access until you remove them (we don't auto-remove) |
| Webhooks + API keys | Kept | Kept |
| Call history | Kept | Kept |

## Failed plan switch

Stripe occasionally fails to process the proration charge (declined card, etc.):

- The plan switch is **rolled back** — you stay on your current plan
- Failed payment notification sent to your billing email
- Fix the payment method, then try the switch again

## Why "Switch Plan" might only show "Cancel"

If you click Switch Plan and the only option is Cancel Subscription (Bug #35), that's a known issue currently in repair. Workaround:

1. Email support@cloudconcierge.ai with the plan you want to switch to
2. We apply the change manually within 1 business day
3. Or wait for the fix to ship (queued for upcoming release)
