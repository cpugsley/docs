## What each FAQ includes

- **Question** (up to 500 characters) — how a customer would phrase it
- **Answer** (up to 2,000 characters) — what the AI should say

## Writing FAQs that work

The AI matches inbound questions semantically (it understands paraphrases). But you'll get better results if you write questions the way customers actually ask them.

**Good example:**
> **Q:** Do you take insurance?
>
> **A:** We accept most major insurance plans including Blue Cross, Aetna, Cigna, and United Healthcare. We're not in-network with Medicare or Medicaid. If you call with your insurance card handy, we can verify coverage in about a minute.

**Less effective:**
> **Q:** Insurance acceptance policies and reimbursement procedures
>
> **A:** Various plans accepted. Inquire at time of booking.

(Customers don't talk like the second one.)

## What to seed

Start with the 10-15 questions your team answers most often on the phone. Common categories:

- **Pricing** ("What does X cost?", "Do you offer payment plans?")
- **Logistics** ("Where are you located?", "Do you do house calls?")
- **Policies** ("What's your cancellation policy?", "Do you take insurance?")
- **Qualifications** ("Are you licensed?", "How long have you been in business?")
- **Edge cases** ("Do you work weekends?", "Do you serve [neighboring town]?")

## Suggest FAQs

Click **Suggest FAQs** to have the AI propose entries based on your business info and services. Review each suggestion before adding — the AI generates starting points, not final answers.

## Editing

- Click any FAQ to edit
- Drag to reorder (affects nothing — order is just for your reference)
- Trash icon to delete (no undo)

## What the AI does with this

When a caller asks a question, the AI checks (in order):

1. **Services** — for capability and pricing questions
2. **FAQs** — for everything else
3. **Custom Instructions** (Settings → General) — for tenant-specific business logic
4. **General knowledge** — for context the AI can answer from common knowledge

If none of the above answer the question confidently, the AI offers to take a message.

<Tip>
The more specific your FAQs, the less the AI relies on general knowledge — which means more accurate, more on-brand answers.
</Tip>
