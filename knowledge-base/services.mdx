Open **Knowledge Base → Services**. The AI uses this list to answer questions like "what do you offer?", "how much does X cost?", and "do you do Y?"

## What each service entry includes

- **Service name** (up to 200 characters) — the customer-facing name
- **Description** (up to 1,000 characters) — what it includes, who it's for, any limitations
- **Price** (free-form text) — "$150 starting", "$89-149", "Call for quote", "Free consultation"

## Writing services well

The AI uses your description verbatim when explaining services. Write for a caller, not a website.

**Good example:**
> **Service name:** Drain cleaning
>
> **Description:** Clears clogged kitchen sinks, bathroom drains, and main sewer lines. Includes a camera inspection if the clog returns within 30 days. Most clogs cleared in under an hour.
>
> **Price:** $150 starting, most jobs $150-300

**Less effective:**
> **Service name:** Plumbing services
>
> **Description:** We do plumbing.
>
> **Price:** Varies

## Auto-populated services

When you sign up via the demo flow (entering your business URL), we scrape your website and pre-populate up to 8 services. Review and edit these — the scraper does its best, but you know your business better.

<Warning>
If services were scraped before May 13, 2026, descriptions may be capped at 300 characters (old limit). We've since increased to 1,000 characters. Edit any truncated entries to add detail.
</Warning>

## Editing and reordering

- Click any service to edit name, description, or price
- Use the drag handle to reorder (the AI mentions services in this order)
- Click the trash icon to delete (no undo — be sure)
- Click **Add Service** to add new entries

## Saving

Changes save individually as you edit. No "Save All" button — each edit persists immediately.

## What the AI does with this

- **Pricing questions:** "How much for a drain cleaning?" → AI quotes from your Price field
- **Capability questions:** "Do you do furnace repair?" → AI checks your Services list, says yes or "we don't, but we recommend calling [partner]" if you've configured a referral
- **Comparison questions:** "What's the difference between basic and deluxe?" → AI uses descriptions to compare

<Tip>
If a caller asks about something you don't offer, the AI will say so honestly and offer to take a message. It won't invent services.
</Tip>
