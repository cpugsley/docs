Open **Knowledge Base → Transfer Rules**. Each rule defines a condition and a phone number. When the AI detects the condition, it tries to transfer the caller live.

## What each rule includes

- **Condition** — natural-language description of when to transfer ("caller is angry", "asking for the owner", "billing dispute over \$500")
- **Transfer to** — destination phone number in E.164 format
- **Available hours** — when this rule is active ("24/7", "9-5 weekdays", "after-hours only")
- **Active** — toggle to enable/disable without deleting

## Example rules

| Condition | Transfer to | Hours | When it fires |
|---|---|---|---|
| Caller is upset or insists on a human | +15551234567 | 24/7 | De-escalation |
| Asking specifically for the owner by name | +15559876543 | 9-5 weekdays | VIP routing |
| Emergency / urgent service request | +15555555555 | 24/7 | On-call routing |
| Press / media inquiry | +15551112222 | 9-5 weekdays | Comms routing |

## Writing conditions

The AI uses fuzzy matching, but specific is better than vague:

- **Good:** "Caller says they need emergency service or that their basement is flooding"
- **Less good:** "Emergency"

The AI evaluates conditions in the order you list them. First match wins. Drag to reorder if precedence matters.

## What the caller experiences on transfer

1. AI says "Let me get you to the team member's name (or 'someone on our team' if unknown) right away."
2. Brief hold music or silence (1-3 seconds)
3. Call rings the configured number
4. If no answer in 20 seconds, the AI picks the call back up and offers to take a message

<Note>
If the destination doesn't answer, the caller doesn't hang up — they come back to the AI. We don't drop callers into voicemail without offering an alternative.
</Note>

## Limitations

- **One-leg transfer only** — you can't transfer from a transferred call (no chaining)
- **No international transfers** at launch
- **Per-minute cost applies** during the transferred portion (same as regular call minutes)

## Saving

Each rule saves individually as you edit. Use the **Active** toggle to disable a rule without deleting it (useful for staff turnover).

## Common gotcha

If you've configured a transfer for "asking for the owner" and the owner is also you (the account owner), make sure the destination number is your actual cell phone, not the CloudConcierge business number — otherwise the call loops back to the AI.

<Warning>
Transfer rules were partially functional in early dev — a bug fix is queued for the upcoming release. If transfers aren't firing in production, check the assigned phone numbers are correct and that the rule is marked Active.
</Warning>
