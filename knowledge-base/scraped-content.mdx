If you signed up via the demo flow (entering your business URL during the AI demo), CloudConcierge **scraped your website** and pre-populated your knowledge base with a starting set of services and FAQs.

This page explains what the scraper does, what to review, and how to handle the inevitable inaccuracies.

## What gets scraped

The scraper visits your website and extracts:

| Field | What it finds | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| **Business name** | From page titles, header, schema.org markup | — |
| **Industry** | Inferred from content | Single category |
| **Services** | Anything that looks like a service offering | Up to 8 services |
| **FAQs** | Q&A patterns, FAQ pages | Up to 10 FAQs |
| **Business hours** | Schema.org `openingHours`, hours pages | Per-day |
| **Contact info** | Phone, email, address | Best-effort |
| **Service area** | "We serve [region]" patterns | Free-form text |

We use **Firecrawl** to crawl up to 20 pages from your site (we hit the most-linked pages first) and **Claude (via AWS Bedrock)** to extract structured data from the unstructured HTML.

## Field limits

To prevent runaway data, each field has a hard limit:

- **Service name:** 200 characters
- **Service description:** 1,000 characters (raised from 300 in May 2026 — see Bug #24)
- **FAQ question:** 500 characters
- **FAQ answer:** 2,000 characters

If the scraper finds more, it truncates.

## What to review (right after signup)

**Open Knowledge Base → Services.** Scan each entry:

1. **Is the name right?** Scrapers sometimes pick up SEO titles like "Best Drain Cleaning Services in Springfield" instead of "Drain Cleaning"
2. **Is the description accurate?** Marketing copy sometimes scrapes in ("We're the #1 plumber in...") — clean these out
3. **Is the price right?** Often missing from websites. Add manually.
4. **Are any services missing?** Add anything the scraper missed.
5. **Any wrong services?** Delete what's not yours.

Then **Knowledge Base → FAQs.** Same review:

1. **Are the questions natural?** Scraper sometimes pulls headings as questions ("Our Services" isn't a question)
2. **Are the answers complete?** Web content often has truncated answers ("Read more")
3. **Add what's missing.** Real phone-call questions tend to differ from web-FAQ questions.

Plan for ~15-30 minutes of cleanup right after signup. This is the single highest-leverage investment in AI accuracy.

## Common scraper hiccups

### Service descriptions cut off mid-sentence
**Cause:** Bug #24 — old 300-char limit. Fixed for new scrapes after May 13, 2026. Existing entries from before that may still be truncated.

**Fix:** edit the service in the Knowledge Base, expand the description manually.

### Wrong industry inferred
**Cause:** scraper sees ambiguous content (e.g., a plumber who also does HVAC may get categorized as HVAC only)

**Fix:** Settings → General → Industry dropdown. Pick the right one.

### Hours look wrong
**Cause:** scraper picked up "by appointment" or seasonal hours

**Fix:** Settings → Business Hours. Manual override.

### Service area mangled
**Cause:** free-form text on the website ("Serving the greater Boston area within 30 miles") gets paraphrased

**Fix:** Settings → General → Service Area. Edit to be precise.

### Phone number scraped, but it's the wrong line
**Cause:** marketing page shows main business number; the scraper grabs it

**Fix:** Settings → General → Contact Phone. Set the line you actually want CC to know about. (Note: this is just for the AI's reference, NOT what callers dial — that's your business phone number.)

## Why scraper accuracy matters

The AI uses Knowledge Base content **verbatim** when answering questions. If the scraper grabbed:

- Marketing fluff → AI sounds like marketing copy on calls (bad)
- Outdated pricing → AI quotes wrong prices (very bad)
- Wrong services → AI books appointments for things you don't offer (very, very bad)

Garbage in, garbage out applies in spades. Spend the 30 minutes reviewing.

## Re-scraping

You can't trigger a fresh scrape from the dashboard (yet). If your website has changed significantly and you want a re-scrape:

1. Contact support@cloudconcierge.ai with your business URL
2. We re-run the scraper and overwrite your knowledge base
3. **Backup your manual edits first** — re-scrape replaces them

In practice, most tenants don't need to re-scrape — they just manually update their KB as their business evolves.

## Scraper limitations

Things the scraper **can't** figure out:

- **Prices** — only on websites that publish prices (most don't)
- **Insurance accepted** — same; rarely listed publicly
- **Specific policies** ("we don't service equipment older than 20 years") — usually not on websites
- **Tone preferences** — you specify these in Custom Instructions
- **Service-specific FAQs** — generic FAQs sometimes; specific operational ones almost never

For all of these, manual entry is required.
