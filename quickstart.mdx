## Before you start

You'll need:
- A business email address
- Your business name, hours, and main services (1-2 sentences each)
- A phone number you want CloudConcierge to forward to OR a number you'd like to provision

## Step 1: Sign up

Go to [app.cloudconcierge.ai](https://app.cloudconcierge.ai) and create an account. Pick a plan (you can change it later).

## Step 2: Build your knowledge base

After signup, you'll land on the dashboard. The AI needs to know about your business before it can answer calls:

1. **Business info** — Settings → General. Add your business name, industry, timezone, contact phone, and a short description.
2. **Services** — Knowledge Base → Services. Add what you offer (e.g., "Drain cleaning — $150 starting", "Furnace repair — diagnostic $89").
3. **FAQs** — Knowledge Base → FAQs. Add the 5-10 questions customers ask most.
4. **Business Hours** — Settings → Business Hours. Set when you're open. The AI mentions hours when relevant.

## Step 3: Pick a voice and greeting

Settings → Voice & Greeting:
- **Voice** — Choose from a curated list of natural voices
- **Main greeting** — How the AI answers ("Thanks for calling Acme Plumbing — how can I help?")
- **Speaking style** — Slider from concise to conversational

## Step 4: Test on your dashboard

Click **"Call me now to test"** on the dashboard. Your AI calls you in seconds.

## Step 5: Go live

Forward your business line to your CloudConcierge number. That's it.

<Tip>
You can run a private test number first before forwarding your live business line. Just keep both for a week to make sure you're happy.
</Tip>
