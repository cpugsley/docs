## Phone & calls

### My AI receptionist isn't picking up

1. Confirm your business phone number is **active** in **Dashboard → Settings → General → Business Phone Number**
2. Confirm your line is forwarded to that number (carrier setting — varies by phone provider)
3. Place a test call to the CloudConcierge number directly — does the AI answer?
   - **Yes**: forwarding is the issue. Check your carrier's call forwarding setup.
   - **No**: contact support@cloudconcierge.ai with your tenant ID

### Recordings won't download

Click the **Download** button on a call detail. If you get a CORS error, that's the old bug — fixed in production as of May 2026. If you still see it:
- Hard refresh the dashboard (Ctrl+Shift+R or Cmd+Shift+R)
- Try a different browser
- Contact support

## Calendar & appointments

### Google Calendar connect fails with "Access Blocked"

This was a known issue with OAuth scopes — fixed. If you see it now, contact support@cloudconcierge.ai.

### Appointment booked twice when caller reschedules

The AI currently creates a new appointment instead of updating the existing one on reschedule (Bug #29 in our tracker). Workaround: manually delete the old appointment from your calendar. Permanent fix queued.

### Booking status buttons don't update in the Bookings dashboard

Fixed as of May 2026 (Bug #27). If you still see stale state, hard refresh.

## Settings

### Speaking style slider resets to 50%

Fixed as of May 2026 (Bug #25). The slider now saves correctly. If you still see resets:
- Confirm you clicked **Save Changes**
- Hard refresh
- If still broken, contact support with a screen recording

### Business hours editor missing

Fixed as of May 2026 (Bug #23). Editor is now in **Settings → General → Business Hours** (below Custom Instructions). If you don't see it there, hard refresh — your browser may be caching an old build.

## Knowledge Base

### Service description is cut off after a few lines

Fixed for new entries as of May 2026 (Bug #24). The limit was raised from 300 to 1,000 characters. **Existing truncated entries** stay truncated until you edit them — go to Knowledge Base → Services → click the entry → expand the description → save.

### "Suggest FAQs" button doesn't do anything

Currently being repaired (Bug #30). Workaround: add FAQs manually. See [FAQs](/knowledge-base/faqs) for guidance.

## Still stuck?

- A short description of what you tried
