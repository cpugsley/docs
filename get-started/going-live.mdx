You've configured services, FAQs, hours, voice — but should you actually flip the switch? Run this checklist first.

## The 12-point pre-launch check

<Steps>
  <Step title="Business name is correct">
    **Settings → General → Business Name** — Does the AI introduce your business correctly? Test by calling your CC number and listening to the greeting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Greeting sounds right">
    **Settings → Voice & Greeting → Main Greeting** — Read it aloud. Does it match how you'd want a real receptionist to answer?
  </Step>
  <Step title="Business hours are accurate">
    **Settings → General → Business Hours** — Verify each day. After-hours greeting should be configured too.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Services cover the top 80% of what customers ask">
    **Knowledge Base → Services** — At minimum, your 5-10 most-requested services should be listed with descriptions and prices.
  </Step>
  <Step title="FAQs answer the 10 most common phone questions">
    **Knowledge Base → FAQs** — "Where are you located?", "What are your hours?", "Do you take insurance?", etc.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Transfer rules route correctly (if applicable)">
    **Knowledge Base → Transfer Rules** — If you want angry callers, owners, or emergencies routed to a real person, configure rules and test them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Calendar is connected (if booking appointments)">
    **Settings → Integrations → Google or Microsoft Calendar** — OAuth complete, primary calendar set. Test by asking the AI to book a slot.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Custom instructions reflect your business voice">
    **Settings → General → Custom Instructions** — Any special rules ("never offer discounts without manager approval", "always ask for account number first") are in there.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test from a friend's phone">
    Don't just test from your own phone — call from a number the AI doesn't know. Verify the AI handles a first-time caller naturally.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test booking flow end-to-end">
    Call, ask to book an appointment, complete the booking. Verify it landed on your calendar with the right details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test edge cases">
    Try saying "I want to speak to a human" — does it transfer? Try asking something you didn't document — does the AI handle it gracefully?
  </Step>
  <Step title="Forward your business line">
    See [Forward your business line](/get-started/forwarding) for carrier-specific instructions.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Recommended soft-launch approach

Forwarding all calls to a new AI on day one is bold. Consider this safer rollout:

### Week 1: Shadow mode
- Run CC number alongside your existing line
- Promote the CC number to friends, family, or beta customers
- Listen to call recordings, refine FAQs and instructions
- Don't forward the real business line yet

### Week 2: Conditional forward (busy/no-answer)
- Forward your business line to CC **only when you can't pick up**
- AI catches the calls you'd otherwise miss
- Lowest-risk way to add CC to your workflow

### Week 3+: Full forward
- Forward all calls to CC
- You become the escalation path, not the front line
- This is when CloudConcierge pays for itself

## Watch list for the first 72 hours

After going fully live, monitor:

- **Dashboard → Calls** — every few hours for the first day. Listen to a few recordings. Are summaries accurate? Are transfers happening when expected?
- **Dashboard → Bookings** — appointments landing on your calendar correctly?
- **Email notifications** — are you getting call summaries? (If not, see [notifications troubleshooting](/troubleshooting/common-issues#notifications))

Plan to spend ~15 min/day in the first week reviewing call activity. After that, you can drop to a weekly review unless something flags up.

## When to escalate to support

Contact support@cloudconcierge.ai if:

- The AI got something materially wrong on a customer call (wrong price, wrong policy, wrong location)
- Bookings disappeared from your calendar
- Forwarding isn't ringing CC despite verification
- Anything that would make a customer angry

Faster than self-service: click the **Get Help** button in the dashboard bottom-right.

<Note>
You don't need to be live 24/7 to launch. Many businesses start by forwarding only outside business hours, then expand to full coverage as confidence builds.
</Note>
