---
title: "Forward your business line"
description: "Route your existing business number to CloudConcierge so callers never know the difference."
---

CloudConcierge provisions a dedicated business phone number for your account, but most customers want their existing business line to ring through to the AI receptionist instead. That's done via **call forwarding** — a setting you configure with your phone carrier.

## The two paths

### Path 1: Forward all calls (recommended)

Forward your entire business line to your CloudConcierge number. Every call goes to the AI receptionist immediately.

### Path 2: Forward conditional (busy / no-answer / unavailable)

Forward only when you can't pick up. You answer when you can; the AI catches everything else.

<Tip>
Most customers start with Path 1 for a week, find the AI handles things well, and never switch back. But Path 2 is a safer way to test if you're nervous.
</Tip>

## Setting up forwarding by carrier

### Major US carriers

Forward-all codes (dial from the line you want to forward):

| Carrier | Enable | Disable |
|---|---|---|
| **AT&T** | `*21*<your-CC-number>#` | `#21#` |
| **Verizon** | `*72<your-CC-number>` | `*73` |
| **T-Mobile** | `**21*<your-CC-number>#` | `##21#` |
| **Sprint** (now T-Mobile) | `*72<your-CC-number>` | `*720` |

Conditional forwarding (busy / no answer):

| Carrier | Busy | No answer |
|---|---|---|
| **AT&T** | `*67*<your-CC-number>#` | `*61*<your-CC-number>#` |
| **Verizon** | `*71<your-CC-number>` | `*71<your-CC-number>` |
| **T-Mobile** | `**67*<your-CC-number>#` | `**61*<your-CC-number>#` |

<Note>
Replace `<your-CC-number>` with your provisioned CloudConcierge number, including area code, no spaces or dashes. Find it in **Settings → General → Business Phone Number**.
</Note>

### Business VOIP providers

If you use a hosted PBX or VOIP service (RingCentral, Vonage, Grasshopper, OpenPhone, etc.), forwarding is configured in their admin panel rather than via star codes:

- **RingCentral:** User Settings → Call Forwarding → forward to external number
- **Vonage:** Phone Settings → Call Forwarding → set forwarding number
- **Grasshopper:** Extensions → Edit → forwarding rules
- **OpenPhone:** Phone Settings → Call Forwarding

Look for "Always", "Unconditional", or "All Calls" forwarding mode and enter your CloudConcierge number.

### Landlines (POTS)

Most landline providers support star codes (`*72` enable, `*73` disable). If those don't work, your phone may require menu-driven forwarding — call your provider for assistance.

## Verifying forwarding works

1. From a different phone (or your cell), dial your business line
2. The AI receptionist should answer with your greeting
3. If your business line rings instead, forwarding isn't active — re-run the enable code

<Warning>
If you forward to CloudConcierge but the AI doesn't answer, **disable forwarding immediately** so customers reach a working line. Then check `Dashboard → Settings → General → Business Phone Number` to confirm your CC number is active.
</Warning>

## Reversing forwarding

To stop forwarding and go back to your line answering directly:

- Dial the disable code for your carrier (table above), OR
- Toggle forwarding off in your VOIP admin panel

You can re-enable any time. CloudConcierge doesn't "own" your business line — your carrier does, you can change forwarding whenever you want.

## What customers see / hear

- Caller dials your existing business number
- Carrier forwards the call silently to your CC number
- AI receptionist answers with your custom greeting
- Caller has no idea it was forwarded

Caller ID may show your business number or the CC number depending on carrier. Most carriers preserve the original (good — keeps your branding).
