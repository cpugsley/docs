---
title: "Business hours"
description: "Tell your AI receptionist when your business is open. Affects greetings, availability statements, and when bookings can land."
---

Open **Settings → General** and scroll to **Business Hours**.

## Setting your hours

For each day Monday through Sunday:

1. **Toggle on/off** — flip the switch to mark the day as Open or Closed
2. **Pick open and close times** — use the time pickers (HH:MM, 24-hour internally; UI shows 12-hour AM/PM)

Default for new accounts: Monday-Friday 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM, weekends closed.

<Tip>
Saturday hours? Toggle Saturday on, set 9:00 AM - 1:00 PM. Closed Tuesdays for vacation? Toggle Tuesday off — your AI will say so when callers ask.
</Tip>

## What your AI does with this

The AI receptionist uses your business hours in several ways:

- **Tells callers when you're open.** If someone asks "are you open?" or "what time do you close?", the AI answers from your configured hours.
- **Switches greetings.** Outside business hours, callers hear your [after-hours greeting](/dashboard/voice-greeting) instead of the main one.
- **Books appropriately.** If you've connected a calendar, bookings only land within open hours unless you override per-appointment-type.

## Common configurations

**Standard 9-to-5:**
- Mon-Fri 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM, weekends closed

**Salon / restaurant:**
- Tue-Sat 10:00 AM - 8:00 PM, Sun 11:00 AM - 4:00 PM, Mon closed

**Home services with on-call after-hours:**
- Mon-Fri 8:00 AM - 6:00 PM, Sat 9:00 AM - 1:00 PM, Sun closed
- (The AI still answers after-hours; the greeting acknowledges it and offers to schedule a callback)

## Saving

Click **Save Business Hours** at the bottom of the section. Changes take effect immediately.

<Note>
Business hours are a soft constraint, not a hard cutoff. Your AI always answers calls 24/7 — business hours just inform what it says and when it books.
</Note>
