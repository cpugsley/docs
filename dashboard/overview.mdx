## Top bar

- **Business name** — your account label, pulled from Settings
- **Phone number badge** — your provisioned business phone number (click to copy)
- **Notifications bell** — unread alerts (red dot indicates new activity)
- **Dark mode toggle** — switch between light and dark themes; preference persists per device
- **User menu** (your initials, top-right) — profile, sign out

## The "Get Help" button

Bottom-right corner of every dashboard page. Click to open a support ticket directly to the CloudConcierge team. Faster than email when you're already in the dashboard.

## What to check daily (first 2 weeks)

1. **Dashboard home** — anything unusual in call volume? Any missed calls?
2. **Recent Calls table** — scan summaries. Any miscategorized? Any that should have transferred but didn't?
3. **Bookings** — anything weird (wrong time slot, duplicate booking)?

## What to check weekly (steady state)

1. **Analytics** — trend lines for volume, sentiment, peak times
2. **Knowledge Base** — review any new questions callers asked that your AI fumbled. Add FAQs accordingly.
3. **Billing** — usage tracking against included minutes; consider plan changes if you're consistently over

## Mobile

The dashboard works on phone and tablet. Some views (admin-only, multi-pane case views) are desktop-optimized; everyday operations work fine on mobile.
