## Time range selector

Top-right, pick a range:
- Last 7 days
- Last 30 days (default)
- Last 90 days
- This month
- Last month
- Custom range

All charts respect the selected range. Hover any chart point for exact values.

## Two tabs

### Overview tab
Big-picture metrics at a glance.

### Reports tab
Deeper dives, per-feature breakdowns, exports.

## Overview charts

### Calls Per Day
Bar chart showing daily call volume. Look for:
- **Spikes** — marketing campaign? Industry event? Bad news (lots of complaints)?
- **Drops** — phone forwarding broken? Holiday slow? Marketing dried up?
- **Trends** — slow climb is good; flat is fine; steady decline needs investigation

### Call Volume by Hour
Heatmap of when calls come in. Use this to:
- Decide your business hours (extend if you're busy after-hours)
- Spot after-hours volume (CC handles it — but maybe humans should be available too)
- Plan staffing if you have humans on-call

### Sentiment Breakdown
Pie chart: 😀 positive / 😐 neutral / 😟 negative.

**Healthy:** ~70% neutral, 20% positive, 10% negative.
**Concerning:** >25% negative — review the negative call transcripts for patterns.

### Top intents
What callers are calling about. Auto-categorized:
- Booking new appointment
- Inquiry (pricing, hours, services)
- Existing appointment (reschedule/cancel)
- Complaint
- Sales/marketing call (yes, these come through too)
- Other

Use this to prioritize what to document better. If 30% of calls are about pricing, your services page needs clearer prices.

## Reports tab

### Average call duration
Trend over time. Watch for:
- **Going up** — AI taking longer to resolve (knowledge base gaps?)
- **Going down** — AI getting more efficient OR callers hanging up sooner (concern if negative sentiment is also up)

### Conversion: calls → bookings
Of calls that should have led to a booking (based on AI-detected intent), what % actually booked?

**Healthy:** 60-80% for service businesses.
**Concerning:** below 50% — calendar issues? Pricing pushback? AI struggling to close?

### Average sentiment score
0-10 scale, trended over time. A single dip is noise. Sustained drop is signal.

### Top FAQs hit
Which of your FAQs are getting the most use. Lets you:
- Move popular FAQs to better placement
- Identify obsolete FAQs (zero hits = consider removing)

### Top failed queries
Questions callers asked that the AI couldn't answer confidently. **This is your prioritized list of FAQs to add.** Treat this like a backlog.

## Exporting

Each chart has an **Export** menu (top-right of the chart):
- **CSV** — raw data for spreadsheets
- **PNG** — chart image for presentations
- **PDF** (Reports tab only) — formatted report

Date range applies to exports.

## Weekly review ritual

Spend 15 minutes every Monday on Analytics:

1. **Calls Per Day** — anything unusual last week?
2. **Sentiment Breakdown** — negative % rising or stable?
3. **Top failed queries** — pick 3 to convert into FAQs this week
4. **Conversion rate** — trending the right direction?

This is one of the highest-leverage uses of your dashboard time.

## Limitations

- **Real-time-ish** — analytics aggregate every 15 minutes, not every call instantly
- **Per-tenant** — you only see your own data. If you're a multi-location business, you'll see all locations combined unless we set up separate tenants
- **No comparison ranges yet** — "this week vs last week" overlay coming soon

For now, eyeball the trend lines or use the date-range selector to compare manually.
