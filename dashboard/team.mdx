Open **Settings → Team**. Add coworkers who need to view calls, manage knowledge base, or access billing.

## Roles

CloudConcierge has three roles:

### Owner
- Full access to everything
- Manage billing, plan changes, cancellation
- Invite/remove team members
- Change tenant-wide settings
- **Each account has exactly one Owner** — the person who signed up. Ownership can be transferred (see below).

### Admin
- Full access except billing
- Can change knowledge base, settings, voice/greeting
- Can manage calls, bookings, integrations
- Can invite/remove **Viewers** but not other Admins
- Cannot access Billing tab

### Viewer
- Read-only access to dashboard, calls, bookings, analytics
- Cannot modify settings, knowledge base, integrations, or billing
- Cannot invite team members
- Good for: bookkeepers, office managers who need visibility but not control

## Plan limits

| Plan | Total team members (including Owner) |
|---|---|
| Starter | 1 (Owner only — no team) |
| Professional | 3 |
| Business | 10 |

Exceeded the limit? Upgrade your plan (Settings → Billing → Switch Plan) or remove an inactive member.

## Inviting a team member

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Invite Member">
    Top-right of the Team page (Owner and Admin only).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter their email">
    Must be a valid email they can access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a role">
    Admin or Viewer (Owners can't invite other Owners).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send invite">
    They get an email with a one-time link to set up their account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Invites expire in 7 days. If unused, send a new one.

## Pending invites

Bottom of the Team page shows invites that haven't been accepted yet. Each has:

- Email invited
- Role assigned
- When sent
- **Resend** button (if delivery may have failed)
- **Revoke** button (cancels the invite)

<Note>

</Note>

## Removing a team member

Click the **⋯** menu next to a member → **Remove**. They lose access immediately. Their personal account isn't affected; only their access to YOUR business account.

## Changing a member's role

Click the **⋯** menu next to a member → **Change role**. Pick Admin or Viewer. Takes effect immediately.

## Transferring ownership

Only the current Owner can transfer:

1. The new Owner must already be an Admin on the team
2. **Settings → Team → ⋯ menu next to the Admin → Make Owner**
3. Confirm with your password
4. Old Owner becomes an Admin automatically (so they still have access)

Useful for: founder transitions, agency clients taking over their own account after onboarding.

## What team members see

- **Owner / Admin:** identical dashboard to yours, including all settings
- **Viewer:** dashboard, calls, bookings, analytics — but settings/knowledge-base tabs hide edit controls (read-only)

Team members never see billing details unless they're Owner.

## Audit trail

We log significant team actions (invites, role changes, removals) — visible to Owner under **Settings → Team → Activity log** (coming soon).

For now, if you suspect unauthorized changes, contact support@cloudconcierge.ai with the timeframe — we can pull the audit trail from server logs.
