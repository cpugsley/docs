## Profile section

- **Full name** — displayed in the dashboard top bar
- **Email** — used for sign-in and account communications
- **Avatar** — uses initials by default; upload a photo for personalization (optional)

Click **Save Changes** after editing.

## Password

Click **Change Password**. You'll be asked for:
- Current password
- New password (min 8 chars, requires uppercase + lowercase + number)
- Confirm new password

Changing your password signs you out of all other devices.

## Email address

To change your sign-in email:
1. Enter the new email in the Email field
2. Click **Save Changes**
3. Check the new email inbox for a verification message
4. Click the link to confirm
5. Old email is retired only after the new one verifies

If the verification email doesn't arrive within 5 minutes, check spam or contact support.

## Two-factor authentication (MFA)

**Account → Security → Enable Two-Factor Authentication.**

Steps:
1. Scan the QR code with an authenticator app (Authy, Google Authenticator, 1Password, Bitwarden)
2. Enter the 6-digit code from the app
3. Save your backup codes (we show them once — store them somewhere safe)
4. MFA is now active on your account

Future sign-ins will prompt for the 6-digit code. Backup codes work if you lose your phone.

<Warning>
If you lose your authenticator AND your backup codes, contact support@cloudconcierge.ai. Account recovery involves identity verification and takes 1-2 business days.
</Warning>

## Notification preferences

**Account → Notifications.** Per-notification-type toggles for how you want to be reached:

| Notification | Email | SMS | In-app |
|---|---|---|---|
| Call summary (every call) | toggle | toggle | always on |
| Caller dropped (mid-call hangup) | toggle | toggle | always on |
| Appointment booked | toggle | toggle | always on |
| Daily digest | toggle | — | always on |
| Weekly report | toggle | — | always on |

<Note>
Notifications system is being repaired (Bug #33). Until the fix ships, in-app dashboard activity is the most reliable signal. See [troubleshooting](/troubleshooting/common-issues#notifications).
</Note>

## Sessions

**Account → Security → Active Sessions.** Lists devices currently signed into your account, with location and last activity.

- Click **Revoke** on a session to sign that device out immediately.
- Revoke all sessions if your account may have been compromised — then change your password and re-enable MFA.

## Deleting your account

CloudConcierge isn't built for self-serve account deletion (yet). To close your account:

1. **Cancel any active subscription** via Billing → Cancel Subscription
2. Email support@cloudconcierge.ai requesting account deletion
3. We'll confirm via email, then process within 7 business days
4. Your data (call recordings, transcripts, settings) is purged within 30 days

If you change your mind during the 30-day window, reach out — we can usually restore.

## Tenant-level vs personal

This page is for **your personal account**. For business-level settings (greetings, hours, services, billing), go to the appropriate Dashboard or Settings section. The user menu only manages YOU; tenant-wide changes happen elsewhere and require Owner role.
