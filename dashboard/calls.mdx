Open **Dashboard → Calls**. Every inbound call (voice or chat) is logged here.

## The call log table

Columns shown by default:

- **Date / Time** — when the call started
- **Caller** — phone number or "Web Visitor" for chat sessions
- **Channel** — `voice` (phone) or `chat` (web widget)
- **Duration** — total call length (excludes hold time before AI pickup)
- **Summary** — one-line AI-generated summary of the conversation
- **Tags** — auto-applied tags (e.g., `booking`, `inquiry`, `complaint`)
- **Sentiment** — 😀 positive · 😐 neutral · 😟 negative (AI-detected)
- **Status** — `Answered`, `Missed`, `Transferred`, `Abandoned`

Click any row to open the **call detail panel**.

## Filtering and search

- **Search bar** — searches caller phone, summary text, and tags
- **Tag filter** — narrow to one tag (e.g., show only `booking`)
- **Channel filter** — `voice` only, `chat` only, or `all`
- **Date range** — pick start/end dates

Filters combine. Pagination shows 25 calls per page by default.

## Call detail panel

When you click a call, the right panel shows:

### Overview tab
- Caller info (number, name if known, location if available)
- Call duration, channel, status
- AI summary (the one-liner that appears in the table, expanded with context)
- Tags applied to this call
- Actions the AI took (booked appointment, looked up customer in CRM, transferred, etc.)

### Transcript tab
Full turn-by-turn transcript:
- **AI** turns shown in blue
- **Caller** turns shown in gray
- Timestamps on each turn

Click any AI turn to see why it said that (it'll show the knowledge source — service, FAQ, custom instruction, etc.) — **coming soon** in a future release.

### Recording tab
- **Play** — embedded audio player
- **Download** — `.wav` file
- **Delete** — remove the recording (transcript stays)

<Note>
Recordings are stored in your AWS S3 bucket per your account. Retention is configurable (default: 90 days). To change, contact support.
</Note>

## Tags

The AI auto-tags calls based on intent:

| Tag | What it means |
|---|---|
| `booking` | Caller booked or attempted to book an appointment |
| `inquiry` | General question, no action taken |
| `complaint` | Caller expressed dissatisfaction |
| `transfer` | Call was transferred to a human |
| `voicemail` | Caller left a voicemail message |
| `urgent` | AI detected urgency keywords |
| `unresolved` | AI couldn't fully resolve the issue |

You can also add your own tags manually (click a tag chip → Edit) for filtering later.

## Sentiment

AI-detected emotional tone:
- **😀 Positive** — caller was satisfied, polite, happy
- **😐 Neutral** — transactional, no strong emotion
- **😟 Negative** — frustrated, confused, angry

Use sentiment to spot patterns: are calls getting worse over time? Is one service category generating more frustration? Check Analytics for trends.

## Status flow

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Answered** | AI handled the call successfully |
| **Missed** | Phone rang but AI didn't pick up (rare — usually a Connect outage) |
| **Transferred** | AI routed to a human per a transfer rule |
| **Abandoned** | Caller hung up mid-conversation |

## Exporting calls

Click the **Export** button (top-right of the table) to download a CSV of the filtered view. Includes caller, summary, sentiment, tags, duration, status. Useful for reporting or feeding into other systems.

## Common workflows

### "I want to listen to today's calls"
- Filter: Date range = today
- Sort by Date descending
- Click each row → Recording tab → Play

### "Where are we losing customers?"
- Filter: Sentiment = 😟 Negative
- Read transcripts to find patterns
- Often reveals missing FAQ or unclear pricing

### "Did we book the appointment for John Doe?"
- Search: "John Doe" in the search bar
- Filter: tag = `booking`
- Open the call → check Actions for "appointment created" + Bookings page for the entry
