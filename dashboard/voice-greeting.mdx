## Greetings

### Main greeting

The first thing callers hear. Keep it short, clear, and on-brand.

**Default:** "Thanks for calling {your business} — how can I help you today?"

**Good examples:**
- "Hi, this is the Acme Plumbing AI assistant — what can I help you with?"
- "Thanks for calling Sunset Dental. I can book appointments or answer questions — what do you need?"

**Avoid:**
- Long intros that delay the caller's reason for calling
- Listing menu options (this isn't an IVR — let the AI handle routing)
- Promotional content ("Our family-owned business has served the community since 1985...")

### After-hours greeting

Used when callers reach you outside your configured business hours. The AI still handles the call — this greeting just acknowledges the time of day.

**Default:** "Thanks for calling {your business}. We're closed right now, but I can still help — what do you need?"

## Speaking style

A slider from **Concise** (0) to **Conversational** (100). The default is 50.

| Setting | What it sounds like |
|---|---|
| **0-25 (Concise)** | Short, efficient responses. "Tuesday at 2pm works. Want me to book it?" |
| **25-50 (Brief)** | Helpful but minimal small talk. Best for high-volume professional services. |
| **50-75 (Conversational)** | Natural back-and-forth with context. Best for hospitality, real estate, salons. |
| **75-100 (Warm)** | Friendly, chatty, adds reassurance. Best for therapy, healthcare, services where rapport matters. |

<Note>
The slider takes effect on your next call. Existing in-progress calls finish on the previous setting.
</Note>

## Saving changes

Click **Save Changes** at the bottom of the Voice & Greeting tab. Changes apply immediately to new inbound calls.
