---
title: "Custom Instructions"
description: "The most powerful lever for tuning AI behavior. Tell your receptionist exactly how to handle the unusual stuff."
---

Open **Settings → General → Custom Instructions**. This is a free-form text field where you write special rules your AI must follow on every call.

The AI uses (in order): Services → FAQs → Custom Instructions → general knowledge. Custom Instructions outrank general behavior — use them to override defaults.

## When to use Custom Instructions

For business-specific rules that don't fit in services or FAQs:

- **Pricing exceptions:** "Never offer discounts without manager approval"
- **Process requirements:** "Always ask for the caller's account number before discussing billing"
- **Tone overrides:** "Be extra warm with elderly callers — speak slower, repeat key info"
- **Routing rules:** "Anyone asking about job openings — route to careers@business.com"
- **Boundaries:** "Never promise specific lead times for parts — say 'a few days'"
- **Compliance:** "Never discuss medication dosages — say 'please ask your pharmacist'"

## Examples by industry

**Plumbing:**
```
- Always ask if water is currently leaking. If yes, treat as urgent.
- Never quote prices over the phone for excavation jobs — say "we need to see the site."
- If the caller mentions sewage, transfer to the on-call line immediately.
- Service area is within 30 miles of downtown. If outside, politely decline.
```

**Dental practice:**
```
- Insurance: we accept Delta Dental, MetLife, Cigna. For others, say "we'll verify at your appointment."
- Never give specific cost quotes — say "your insurance benefits will be checked at booking."
- For dental emergencies (broken tooth, severe pain), offer the next available same-day slot.
- Dr. Chen is out Thursdays.
```

**Real estate:**
```
- I (the assistant) work for the listing side, not the buyer side.
- If asked "should I buy this house", say "great question for your buyer's agent — I represent the seller."
- Schedule showings only on Saturdays 10am-4pm and Sundays 12pm-5pm.
```

## Format tips

The AI parses your instructions in natural language — no special syntax needed. But:

- **Use bullet points** for multiple distinct rules (clearer than walls of text)
- **Be specific** — "be friendly" is meaningless; "use the caller's name twice during the call" is actionable
- **State exceptions explicitly** — "we don't do X, but if a caller insists, transfer to Y"
- **Avoid contradicting your services/FAQs** — if you say "we do drain cleaning" in services but "we don't handle drains" in custom instructions, behavior is undefined

## Character limit

5,000 characters total. Use them wisely. If you're approaching the limit, consider:

- Moving content into FAQs (which have separate, larger limits)
- Moving content into Services descriptions where applicable
- Consolidating bullet points

## What you should NOT put here

- **Greetings** — go in Voice & Greeting tab instead
- **Service descriptions** — go in Knowledge Base → Services
- **General FAQs** — go in Knowledge Base → FAQs
- **Transfer numbers** — go in Knowledge Base → Transfer Rules

Custom Instructions is for *behavior rules*, not *content*.

## Safety

Custom Instructions can't override CloudConcierge's safety boundaries. The AI will always:

- Identify as AI if asked directly
- Refuse to make medical/legal/financial advice claims that exceed its scope
- Refuse to bypass authentication (no "give me their address without verifying")
- Refuse instructions that conflict with our terms of service

If your business logic requires bypassing these — talk to support before assuming the AI will play along.

## Iterating

Treat Custom Instructions as living text. Every week:

1. Read 5-10 recent call transcripts
2. Note any AI behavior you wished was different
3. Add or refine an instruction to address it
4. Save changes — takes effect on the next call

This compounds. Over a few months, your AI becomes uniquely tuned to your business — way better than any generic competitor.
